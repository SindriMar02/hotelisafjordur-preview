The rooms at Torg

Thirty-six rooms above the desk and the restaurant. Every one has its own bathroom, free WiFi and a TV, and the windows look over Pollurinn or up the fjord to Snæfjallaströnd. The upper floors have a lift.

Pick your nights

Torg is the hotel on the square, with Logn and the reception on the ground floor. The rooms are bright and plainly furnished, with a view either over Pollurinn, the still water in front of town, or out over Ísafjarðardjúp and Snæfjallaströnd on the far side. Happy hour at Logn is every day from four to six. There is free parking at the hotel and an airport shuttle for a fee.
