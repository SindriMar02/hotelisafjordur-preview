Mánagata 5 · the old town

Gamla Gistihúsið

The old guesthouse, a timber house on Mánagata a few minutes from the square. Nine rooms for nineteen guests over two floors, every one with its own washbasin, and good shared bathrooms on each floor. Open all year.

Pick your nights

A guesthouse on two floors in the middle of Ísafjörður, with seven twin rooms and two triples. There is free WiFi through the house, a shared sitting room with a television upstairs, a book corner where guests compare their maps and write their postcards, and a kitchen with what you need to cook. Children are welcome, and a cot or a mattress can be had for the youngest. Check-in and breakfast are at Torg, from seven to ten.
